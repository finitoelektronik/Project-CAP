# Year-end closing, opening journals, and audit snapshots

Year-end close is performed only by a master account. It reverses the annual balances of every posting account in the Laba-Rugi and HPP groups into `03.04.00.00.00.0000 — LABA RUGI DITAHAN`, then creates a balanced `SA_<year>` journal on 1 January for non-zero Neraca accounts.

The general ledger starts at zero only on that January date when an `SA_` journal exists; the SA stays visible as the first mutation. This avoids both hiding the audit journal and counting it twice.

Closing records persist an anomaly snapshot. A re-close removes only journals generated by the previous close, preserves user and legacy journals, and refreshes the snapshot. Closed periods reject ordinary journals and stock documents; only a new adjustment journal may be posted before re-closing.
